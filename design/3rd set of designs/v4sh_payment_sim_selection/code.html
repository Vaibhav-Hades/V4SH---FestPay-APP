Why do I need to choose a SIM?

Some supported payment flows use your mobile network connection. Selecting a SIM helps V4SH use the intended cellular connection when required.

You can change your payment SIM later from V4SH settings.

SIM selection does not modify your bank account or registered UPI credentials.
