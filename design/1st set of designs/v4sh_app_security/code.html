Authentication happens through your payment provider

V4SH does not collect, store, or simulate your UPI PIN or banking passwords. V4SH will never ask for your UPI PIN, banking password, card PIN, CVV, or OTP. Every transaction is authenticated securely inside your payment provider's cryptographic boundary.

privacy_tip Stay secure
  • Never share your UPI PIN or one-time verification codes with anyone.
  • Never approve a collect request or authorization you did not deliberately initiate.
  • Keep your device screen lock enabled with biometric or a strong passcode.
  • Verify merchant and recipient names carefully before authorizing funds transfer.

V4SH is an interface layer for your UPI payments. Your payment provider and issuing bank remain exclusively responsible for authenticating, processing, and settling your transactions.
