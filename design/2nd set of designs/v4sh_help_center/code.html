Payment status unclear What to do when confirmation hasn't arrived
arrow_forward

During intermittent network moments, banking servers may take up to 2-3 minutes to acknowledge an API handshake. Please check your official bank SMS or primary banking ledger before re-initiating the transfer to avoid accidental double debiting.

No mobile network Understanding connectivity requirements
arrow_forward

V4SH can scan and cache payment intents without continuous data connectivity, but executing cryptographic bank settlement requires an active cellular data or Wi-Fi connection with your payment service provider.

arrow_forward

Ensure adequate ambient light and align the printed merchant QR code within the viewfinder corners. V4SH screens codes for valid NPCI UPI uri formats prior to handoff.

arrow_forward

Failures typically stem from issuing bank server timeouts, reaching daily transactional limits, or an incorrect UPI PIN. Check your account balance directly within your banking provider application.

Payment cancelled What happens after cancelling a payment
arrow_forward

If you abort before entering your UPI PIN, no debit occurs. If an external UPI app was cancelled mid-flight, verify your debit statement to ensure funds remained untransferred.
