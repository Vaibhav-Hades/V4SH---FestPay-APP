## Permissions and external boundaries

Merged release requests CALL_PHONE, CAMERA, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (transitive) and the app's signature-protected receiver permission. The service requires BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE from Android. No INTERNET, SMS, contacts, location, broad storage, root or private APIs are used by V4SH. The app UID cannot directly use internet sockets without INTERNET; this is not an exhaustive audit of vendor/system-service IPC or a legal determination that no data is collected.

The carrier/bank, Android system, camera/decoder SDK and user-selected sharing apps are distinct boundaries. V4SH does not collect PIN values; the user enters the PIN in the carrier interface. Do not capture that screen for testing. QR names are requester-supplied; Receive displays the saved UPI ID rather than linking a bank account.

## Retention, deletion and restore facts

No production retention period, export/deletion workflow, recovery-key service or approved reconciliation-assisted deletion policy is implemented. Those need named ownership and privacy/provider review. Do not add a destructive “clear unresolved history” control. Android clear storage/uninstall can remove application data and keys; this is data loss, not cancellation, failure, settlement or permission to repay.

Backup is disabled; cloud/device-transfer XML excludes preferences, databases, files and root data, and seals are in no-backup storage. Fault tests reject missing keys/seals, incomplete writes and partial rollback. Actual cloud/device-transfer and all-OEM behavior remain NOT TESTED. There is no forensic erasure guarantee for old flash blocks or third-party copies. Full rollback of all integrity locations is outside the software seal's guarantee.

## Privacy review inputs still required

Owner must set supported regions/users, legal basis/notices, named privacy/support/incident contacts, retention schedules, provider access scope and user-rights/recovery procedures. Review exact SDK versions and outbound behavior before selecting Play Data Safety answers; no declaration was submitted here. A future networked provider adapter would require a new inventory, disclosure, credential/access-control and retention review.

Primary vendor evidence: [ML Kit terms/privacy](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) describes on-device input/output processing and separate metrics behavior; [ML Kit Android disclosure guidance](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) lists diagnostic metadata categories. These vendor statements do not replace examination of this artifact or privacy-owner decisions.
