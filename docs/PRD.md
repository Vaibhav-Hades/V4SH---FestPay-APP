# V4SH Product Requirements

Status: Proposed freeze baseline; review and approval remain Phase 2 entry requirements. These documents specify target behavior, not implemented guarantees. Production payment confirmation is blocked.

## Product purpose and target user

V4SH aims to assist users in initiating supported cellular USSD payments and tracking the outcome honestly, including when mobile internet or Wi-Fi is unavailable. Intended users have a bank-registered SIM, an eligible account, and a supported device/carrier/bank combination. Eligibility and permission to assist the intended `*99#` route remain unverified; no supported production matrix is claimed.

## Core problem and product promise

Launching a dialer, navigating menus, authenticating, submitting an instruction, and confirming a completed payment are separate events. Loss of the USSD session does not establish whether money moved.

The target promise is a durable record of what V4SH knows and cannot establish. PAYMENT INITIATION means beginning an external payment interaction. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION requires trustworthy, transaction-specific evidence from an authorized source. Initiation is never sold as confirmation.

## CURRENT STATE

The validated Android prototype uses Compose, CameraX/ML Kit QR input, telephone intents, an AccessibilityService, and SharedPreferences history. `RealUssdPaymentEngine` returns `AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED` after fixed delays. No authoritative confirmation adapter was demonstrated. Raw accessibility text is logged before filtering. Attempts are not durably created before execution, and duplicate guards are in memory. Runtime-selectable simulation shares the history path.

## TARGET STATE: supported scenario and journey

1. Validate eligibility and payment input; unsupported configurations stop before execution.
2. Show recipient, amount, currency, and selected route/SIM for explicit review and authorization.
3. Atomically create an immutable local attempt and acquire the submission reservation.
4. Persist that external interaction may begin before invoking the execution adapter.
5. Assist only permitted non-sensitive interactions. The user handles authentication in the provider-controlled flow.
6. Persist observations without equating them to financial outcomes.
7. Show verified success or definitive failure only when the evidence policy permits it. Otherwise preserve UNKNOWN and advise checking official bank history before paying again.
8. Recover unresolved attempts on restart without resubmission.

The conditional verified-success journey is unavailable until the verification dependency is satisfied. An initiation-only product may be considered only with explicit acceptance of UNKNOWN and all applicable safety and distribution gates.

## Explicit non-goals

- No invented bank, NPCI, or PSP API; no claim of settlement from local state or screen text.
- No automatic PIN entry, PIN simulation, or collection of authentication secrets.
- No automatic retry, guaranteed external idempotency, or guaranteed control of arbitrary OEM USSD menus.
- No bank statement, custody service, refund engine, or reversal processing in the attempt lifecycle.
- No production payment results generated by simulations.

## Target safety guarantees

These are acceptance obligations, not current assurances: no SUCCESS without approved evidence; uncertainty persists; every external interaction has a pre-existing durable attempt; local IDs are distinct from provider references; unresolved attempts block new payment submission; cancellation claims require proven non-execution; no secret-capable content enters logs or storage.

## Reliability and privacy requirements

Journal write failure blocks execution. Process death, repeated events, timeouts, service interruption, and restore cannot erase uncertainty or cause replay. UI reads durable attempt projections by ID. Payment and balance operations have separate session owners and serialized access to the telephony resource. Financial records use defined protection, retention, and deletion policies; unresolved reservations cannot disappear through ordinary history deletion.

## Production readiness criteria

Evidence-gated state transitions, durable recovery, duplicate prevention, secret protection, truthful receipts, production/test isolation, backup controls, release checks, provider permission, distribution eligibility, and documented device/carrier/bank tests must all have reviewed evidence. A document or passing timer test does not satisfy a release gate.

Payment-confirming release additionally requires an authorized, technically accessible confirmation mechanism with validated authenticity, correlation, and finality semantics. Initiation-only release requires explicit product approval of the restricted scope and honest UNKNOWN behavior. Phase 2 entry is not production approval.

## UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS

Which institutions permit this assistance? Which devices, SIM routes, banks, languages, and carriers are supported? Which authorized source can confirm completion without data connectivity? What are its failure, pending, reversal, and finality semantics? Who supports disputed or unresolved payments? What retention and recovery policies are permitted? These require evidence and named accountable owners during review.
